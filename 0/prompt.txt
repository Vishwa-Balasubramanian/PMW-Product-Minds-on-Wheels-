**What it does**
- **Conflicts chip:** sits next to Quick wins and works the same way (`?q=conflict`, `scroll={false}`). For G70 it shows "Conflicts 3" for All markets, China and Europe, and "Conflicts 0" for the US. - **"Global or market-specific?" block:** appears on conflict cards in the `AlreadySellBanner` spot. It shows:

  - China and Europe as two bars on a 0–10 scale, e.g. 7.69 vs 9.69, gap 2.00, China lower
  - each market's share of 2025 sales (23.8% / 31.0%)
  - the study source line

- **Scope buttons:** "Approve as global requirement" and "Approve as &lt;market&gt;-specific", where the market is the one with the lower score. There is no schema change.
- **After approval:** the block shows the recorded scope, who decided and when, read from the latest approval event. If the card was approved with the general Approve button, it says no scope was recorded. On rejected cards the scope buttons are hidden.
- **Region privacy:** the block only receives survey means, sales shares and the approval details. No customer text reaches it.
