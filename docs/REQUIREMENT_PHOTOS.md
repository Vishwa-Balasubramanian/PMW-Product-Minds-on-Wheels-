# Requirement photo mapping

Quick wins select a photo from their actual feature, rather than a representative topic that may be broader. Ventilated and massage seats now use seating imagery; heated steering wheels use the steering wheel; surround view uses the assistance context. Roominess and rear entertainment take precedence over broad interior/seat rules, and Highway assistant no longer selects a voice-assistant photo.

All equipment glossary features, merged themes, and the current market-conflict subjects resolve to existing local photos and focal points. G70 cluster and speaker coordinates were checked against the supplied cockpit photo. No external image requests or invented product images are introduced.

Asset limitations remain: F70 lacks separate seat and speaker close-ups; rear entertainment, rear roominess and sun protection use the nearest available interior or vehicle context rather than claiming a photograph of the exact hardware. The existing source imagery is kept unchanged.
